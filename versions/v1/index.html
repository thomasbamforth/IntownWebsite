Your Privacy

You're always
in control.

Location sharing is entirely on your terms. Turn it off anytime, choose your radius, and only share with people you trust.

🎯
Approximate location only
Friends see a radius you control — not your exact address. Never your precise GPS.
👥
Friends only — no strangers
Only mutual friends can see you. Strangers can never find your location.
⚡
One tap to disappear
Pause sharing instantly. No one is notified. No awkward conversations needed.
