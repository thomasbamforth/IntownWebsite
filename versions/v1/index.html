Privacy

Your location.
Your rules.

We built InTown knowing that sharing your location requires real trust. Here's exactly what we do — and don't do.

🎯
Approximate radius only — never your exact address
Friends see roughly where you are. Not your home, not your GPS pin.
👥
Mutual friends only — strangers can't find you
Both people have to accept to see each other. No one-way tracking.
👻
One tap to go invisible — no notification sent
Pause at any moment. Nobody knows. No awkward explanations needed.
🛡️
No location history — we don't store where you've been
When you're not sharing, nothing is recorded. Period.
