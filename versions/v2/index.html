Features

Everything proximity
should be.

Designed to bring people together with as little friction as possible.

01
Real-time friend map
See which friends are nearby on a live map. Not "recently seen" — actually right now, in real time.
02
Instant nearby alerts
Get a notification the moment a friend enters your radius. No manual checking. It just works.
03
You choose your radius
Set sharing to half a mile, 2 miles, or anything in between. Control exactly how close "nearby" means.
04
One-tap ghost mode
Need some alone time? Disappear from everyone's map instantly. No notification sent, no awkward explanations.
