Real Moments, Real Connections

InTown shines in the moments that matter - when a friend is closer than you think and all you need is a nudge.

College Reunion, Zero Planning

Your college roommate's flight just landed in your city. InTown notifies you the moment they enter your radius - before they even text.

Why it works: Radius detection fires automatically, so no one has to coordinate over text first.

Spontaneous Friday Night

Plans fell through. InTown shows three friends within a mile right now. One tap and the night is saved.

Why it works: Knowing who's physically close turns "maybe later" into "meet me in 10."

Lost at a Festival

You split up at a music festival. Instead of frantic calls, you open the map and see exactly which friends are within 500 feet.

Why it works: A shared live map beats 20 texts when the signal's weak and the music's loud.

Conference Networking

At a multi-day industry conference, see which mutual connections are in the same building. Turn a badge swap into a real coffee chat.

Why it works: Proximity awareness makes warm outreach feel natural, not forced.

Road Trip Convoy

Your friend caravan is crossing three states. InTown's live map keeps every car in the convoy without a single group-text update.

Why it works: One glance at the map replaces 50 "where are you?" messages.

New City, Hidden Connections

Visiting a city for the first time? InTown shows which of your friends are locals - or also visiting - and ready to be your guide.

Why it works: Turns any trip into a social opportunity hidden in plain sight.
